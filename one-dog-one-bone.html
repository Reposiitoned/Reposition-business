One Dog, One Bone

Most business owners struggle with trying to be everything to everyone. They watch inconsistent referrals, price resistance, and slow pipelines eat away at their margins, assuming it's an inevitable part of running a firm.

The standard move is to broaden your services and widen your target market to capture more opportunities. But doing that only dilutes your messaging and confuses the very people you want to attract. It's time to shut doors and open roofs.

Here's what actually works: give the dog one bone, not five. When you shift from "we help almost anyone with X" to a single, sharply defined ideal client, everything changes. Referrers instantly know who to send you, prospects self-select before the first call, and your fees stop needing defense. Specificity isn't a limitation. It's what allows a boutique firm to out-compete other businesses ten times its size.

Do this today: Write down the one type of client you do your absolute best work for, then remove every other client type from your primary homepage headline. Execute this single lever, and your incoming inquiries will immediately match your highest-margin work.
